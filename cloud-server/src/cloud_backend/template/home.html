Welcome to LettuVault

The ultimate Edge-to-Cloud architectural bridge for Smart Agriculture. Monitoring real-time environmental data and AI crop diagnostics securely from the edge.

🌡️
Precision IoT Sensing
Active monitoring embedded via BME280 sensors. Tracking crucial metrics like Temperature, Humidity, and Pressure to maintain an absolute ideal microclimate.
🍓
Edge AI Vision Model
Utilizing offline YOLOv11 Computer Vision to automatically identify crops (Lettuce/Strawberry) and actively search for wilting or worm pest infestations.
☁️
Hybrid Cloud Mirror
Seamless and robust background synchronization engine executing on the Raspberry Pi edge, keeping this cloud server completely up to date even after network dropouts.
Cloud Mirror Database Online & Listening

The Developer Team

We are 4th year PLM students at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila, taking Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering.

We created LettuVault as our capstone thesis project to bridge the gap between traditional agricultural practices and modern hardware capabilities. Driven by a passion to solve real-world problems through IoT and AI, our goal is to empower farmers and smart greenhouses with accessible, highly autonomous, and data-driven crop analysis systems.
